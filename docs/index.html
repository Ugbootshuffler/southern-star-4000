Southern Star 4000 is a retro, 1990s cable-TV style local weather display for Australia. It is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Bureau of Meteorology or The Weather Channel.

Built on the open-source (MIT) WeatherStar 4000+ by Matt Walsh and the international fork by Michael Wood.
